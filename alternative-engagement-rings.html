Alternative Engagement Rings

An alternative engagement ring is any ring that starts from what you actually love, not from a showroom case of near-identical diamond solitaires. It might be a Montana sapphire the color of a lake at dusk, a salt-and-pepper diamond with a galaxy inside it, or a fantasy-cut stone that catches light in a way a standard cut never will. At Bohemi, every ring is designed around its stone and made to be worn for a lifetime. Different is better, and it always has been.

Bohemi is a Boulder design studio for people who want the opposite of off-the-shelf. Each ring is designed by a gem expert and built around one stone. Because no two stones are alike, no two rings come out exactly the same, even when the design is one we have made before. You are not picking a finished ring off a shelf. You are choosing the stone it gets built around, and that is what makes it yours.

What does it mean to design a ring around the stone?

The stone is the heart of the ring, so it comes first. We source and select the center stone with you, and the design follows it. In a bespoke piece that means drawing from scratch around the stone. In a ready-made or made-to-measure piece it means fitting an existing design to the stone you chose. That order matters either way. It is what lets the metal, the profile, and the setting serve the stone instead of fighting it, and it is why no two Bohemi rings come out exactly the same.

It also means you are guided by someone who knows stones deeply: which ones belong in a ring worn every day, which ones do not, and why. That guidance is the real difference between a ring that dazzles in photos and one that is still whole in thirty years.

Is every Bohemi ring one of a kind?

The stone always is. The design sometimes is, and the honest answer depends on which ring you’re buying. Some Bohemi rings are true one-offs: made once, around a stone that can’t be found again, and when that ring sells, it’s gone. Others are designs Heather can build again, even though the stone in them never repeats. A lab diamond can be matched exactly. A particular Montana parti sapphire cannot.

Both are honest, and neither is lesser. What stays true across all of it is that the stone is chosen for you and the ring is built around that stone, so even a design we have made before does not come out the same twice. If it matters to you that nobody else can ever have your ring, say so at the start and we will build you one that cannot be repeated.

Most alternative engagement rings begin with one of these. Each has its own page, and each is worth understanding before you choose.

Fantasy cuts

Concave precision faceting on a shallow pavilion. It holds an even brightness from many angles as your hand moves, shows a larger face for its carat weight, and sits lower and more securely for everyday wear. Not more sparkle. A stone that stays alive in real light.

Should you choose a lab diamond or a natural one?

Beyond these, we work in spinel, and in both lab and natural diamonds. A lab diamond, also called a synthetic diamond, is a real diamond, chemically and optically identical to a mined one, and usually many thousands less, which is why most couples choose it. Some choose a synthetic to sidestep sourcing concerns, and some want a natural stone specifically. There is no single right answer, only the one that fits your budget and your values. We will talk it through honestly, including which softer stones we steer away from for a ring you will wear every day.

Will an alternative engagement ring hold up to daily wear?

Every Bohemi ring is designed to survive a real life. That is a standard, not a slogan. There is enough metal around every stone to hold it safely, never thinner than half a millimeter at the setting, because a ring you have to baby is not a ring you can actually live in. The trend of a giant stone on a wisp of a band photographs beautifully but does not hold up, so we do not make it. A substantial stone gets a band substantial enough to balance and protect it.

This is also where honest guidance earns its keep. Some stones are softer than they look and are prone to chipping or clouding with daily wear, and we will tell you before you fall for one, not after. The goal is never the sale. The goal is a ring that is still yours, and still whole, a lifetime from now.

Where do the stones and metals come from?

Every sapphire we set is natural, the only treatment ever used is heat, and we always disclose it. Our Montana sapphires can be traced to the source, to named grounds like Rock Creek, Yogo Gulch, Dry Cottonwood Creek and the Missouri River gravel bars. Because they are mined in the United States, they come from operations held to regulated labor and environmental standards, which is a real part of why couples who care where a stone comes from choose them.

Salt-and-pepper diamonds carry no grading report, and that is not a gap. That system was built to score eye-clean, colorless stones, not a diamond whose whole character is what sits inside it. Our white and colored diamonds are sourced through Kimberley Process compliant channels. Our gold is casting grain produced from reclaimed scrap.

Where we cannot claim something, we do not. You will not see a certification here that we do not hold, and you will not be told a stone is untreated when the honest answer is heat, always disclosed.

How much does an alternative engagement ring cost, and how long does it take?

It begins with a free consultation, in the Boulder studio or virtually, with no sales pitch. From there we source your stone, design around it in three to five concepts with room to revise, and price it transparently so you always know the full cost before the next step. One promise we hold to: we work under your top-end budget, not over it.

There are three ways in, depending on how custom you want to go:

Ready-madeAn existing design with a stone from the catalog, from around $4,000.4 to 6 weeks
Made-to-measureAn existing design adapted to you, roughly $6,000 to $10,000.6 to 8 weeks
BespokeA fully custom piece designed from scratch, generally $8,000 to $15,000 and up.8 to 12 weeks

Most couples land between $7,000 and $15,000, and the stone is the biggest lever. If you have a date in mind, it is best to start a few months ahead, and longer if we are sourcing a rare stone for you.

The hardest part is usually saying what you want, because most people are not jewelers and do not have the vocabulary for it. That translation is the real craft here: you bring a feeling, a few saved images, a vibe, and we turn it into a real design that lasts. Clients tend to describe it the same way.

Questions people ask first

What is an alternative engagement ring?
It is a ring built around what you love rather than a standard diamond solitaire. That usually means a distinctive center stone (a fantasy cut, a Montana sapphire, a salt-and-pepper diamond, a colored stone), a one-of-a-kind design, or both. At Bohemi, every ring is designed around its stone. A bespoke piece is designed for one person and never repeated.
Are lab diamonds real diamonds?
Yes. A lab diamond, also called a synthetic diamond, is chemically and optically identical to a mined diamond. The only difference is that one is grown in a lab and the other is pulled from the earth. It usually costs many thousands less, which is why most couples choose it. One honest note: lab diamonds have essentially no resale value, so buy one to wear and keep.
Are the inclusions in a salt-and-pepper diamond flaws?
No. Those black and white flecks are natural characteristics, and every stone’s pattern is one of a kind. A salt-and-pepper diamond is still a full-hardness diamond at a 10 on the Mohs scale, so it stands up to daily wear, and it lives outside the commodity white-diamond market, so you get more stone for your budget.
Does a fantasy cut sparkle more than a standard cut?
Not exactly, and that is the honest answer. A standard brilliant cut actually scintillates more. What a fantasy cut gives you is an even brightness that holds from many angles as your hand moves, a larger face for its carat weight, and a lower, more secure setting for everyday wear.
Where do your sapphires come from?
They are all natural, only ever heat treated, with treatment always disclosed. Most carry mine-level traceability back to a specific Montana mine, which is rare in the industry. We also love sapphires from Australia for their teal-yellow color zoning, Madagascar’s Royal Blue, Tanzania’s vivid purples, and Nigeria’s unmatched saturated greens.
Can I design something completely my own?
Yes, and you do not need the words for it. You bring the feeling and a few images, and we translate that into a real design built to last. The studio is by appointment, so your consultation time is entirely yours.
Can you use a stone I already own?
Yes. Resetting an heirloom stone into something new is part of what we do, alongside men’s pieces, wedding bands, and stacks built to sit together.
