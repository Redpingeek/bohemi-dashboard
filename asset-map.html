Bohemi Round Two

Your new pages, and where they go

What each new piece is, the job it does, and where it should be linked from on your site.

Three new pages are written and waiting for your read. They work as a set. One broad page covers the whole idea of an alternative engagement ring, and two narrower pages go deep on the two stones people search for by name. The broad page sends visitors to the narrow ones, and the narrow ones send visitors back up. That linking is the part that makes them work as a group rather than as three separate pages.

Why these three

What your search numbers say right now

These come from Google Search Console for the four weeks ending August 25, 2026. An impression means Google showed a Bohemi page in someone's search results. The average position is roughly where on the results list you appeared, where 1 to 10 is the first page.

  • "alternative engagement ring stones": 122 impressions, average position about 33. People are searching this and Google is already showing you. Position 33 is about the fourth page of results, and almost nobody scrolls that far. The broad page exists to move that.
  • Salt-and-pepper diamonds: zero impressions in four weeks. Not a small number, zero. You sell these stones and Google is not showing you for the term at all, because there is no page on your site about them. That is what the new page fixes.
  • Montana sapphire: three impressions in four weeks, all for very specific shape searches. Same story, same fix.
Two doors, and they no longer open together. There are now two separate ways a person finds you. One is Google, where someone types a search and clicks a result. The other is an AI assistant, where someone asks ChatGPT or Perplexity or Google's own AI answer to recommend a studio, reads the answer, and comes straight to you. The numbers above only describe the first door. Nothing that happens at the second door appears in them at all. Both doors matter, and these three pages are built to open both, which is why they read like real answers to real questions rather than pages stuffed with search terms.
What a zero here does not mean. A zero in Google's numbers does not mean nobody came. A visit that arrives because an AI assistant recommended you never appears in Google's numbers, so it is invisible on this page no matter how many people it brings. Your rings also sell in person and by private invoice, so a page that Google records almost no activity for can still be the reason someone walked through your door. Read everything above as a map of where you cannot yet be found, and nothing more than that.

What to put on your Home page

Three things, in this order of importance:

  • A link to the broad Alternative Engagement Rings page, high up. This is the single most valuable link on your site. It tells Google and AI assistants that this page is the one that matters, and it catches visitors who arrive knowing only that they want something that is not a plain solitaire.
  • A row of three, linking to the broad page and the two stone pages. Images and a few words each. This gives a visitor who does not know your collections a way in based on the stone they are drawn to, which is how most of them actually decide.
  • The stone matcher, further down. For the visitor who is still browsing. It gives them something to do other than leave.
