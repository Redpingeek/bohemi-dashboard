The thinking behind it

The authority pages

When someone asks Google or an AI assistant for an alternative engagement ring, the recommendation goes to whoever owns that category. Right now that is not you, even though it is exactly what you do. The hub makes Bohemi the answer for the whole category, and the two deep-dive pages catch the specific searches your buyers already make, salt-and-pepper diamonds and Montana sapphires, that you were not yet showing up for. It puts the authority you already earned to work on the terms you own outright.

The stone matcher

Most of your buyers arrive overwhelmed, going off a feeling with no jewelry vocabulary. The quiz meets them right there, asking about color, light, and lifestyle instead of cut and clarity, and it hands you a decoded brief of what they are picturing. It does the first pass of the translation you are so good at, so the consultation starts warm instead of cold, for both of you.

Image accessibility, handled

What we found

We went through every image on your site. The good news: most already carry alt text, the short description a screen reader reads aloud and Google reads too, because you add it as you post, and you write it well. Only a set of images had that field left blank. Those are the ones we are closing.

Your product photos, done in one pass

For your product images we filled every blank description in a single bulk update, drawn straight from your catalog so each one is accurate: the piece, the metal, the form. Nothing for you to click through. This was the most exposed part, and it is closed.

Your blog images stay in your voice

Here is what today taught us. Your blog descriptions are richer than anything automated could write. "Champagne salt and pepper emerald cut diamond in 14k yellow gold inside a pink flower" is yours, and it should stay yours. So you keep writing blog alt as you publish, the way you already do, and we simply catch the few that slip through so nothing sits empty.

The rhythm going forward

When you add an image, add a line describing it, which you already do naturally. On a regular schedule we scan the whole site and hand you a short list of anything left blank, with the exact spot to find it, so there is no hunting and no guessing which image is missing. Product photos we can refresh in bulk anytime from your catalog.

Your fail-safe, for the weeks you are slammed

We also built a tool that can fill blank descriptions automatically, in plain, accurate language. Think of it as a safety net, not the author. If you get busy and cannot get to a batch of missing alt, it makes sure nothing stays empty and exposed. When you have the time, your words always win. It is the backup that keeps this from ever piling up again.
