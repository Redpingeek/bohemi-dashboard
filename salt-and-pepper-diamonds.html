Mother nature's fingerprint

A salt-and-pepper diamond is a real diamond with its story written under the surface: flecks of black, white, red, orange and brown scattered through the stone like a night sky, no two alike. A traditional jeweler would call those inclusions flaws. We call them characteristics, mother nature's fingerprint, and they are exactly the point. If you want a diamond that could only ever be yours, this is where alternative engagement rings begin.

At Bohemi, every salt-and-pepper diamond is chosen with you, designed to suit it, then built to be worn for a lifetime. This is one of the stones we know deeply, and because no two are ever alike, the stone is what makes a ring feel one of a kind, even when the design is not a bespoke one.

A white diamond is graded to be as clear and colorless as possible, which means one looks much like the next. A salt-and-pepper diamond is the opposite. Its inclusions are what make it singular, so choosing one is less like picking a grade off a list and more like finding the one stone whose pattern speaks to you. Some are soft and smoky, some are crisp and graphic, some hold a single dramatic mark. There is no better or worse, only the one that is yours.

Are salt-and-pepper diamonds durable enough for everyday wear?

A salt-and-pepper diamond is a real diamond, which means it sits at a 10 on the Mohs scale, the hardest natural material there is, so it will not scratch in everyday wear. Toughness is the honest nuance: a heavily included stone can be more prone to chipping where an inclusion reaches the surface, and this is exactly where a gem expert's eye earns its place. We hand-select each stone for soundness and favor a protective setting, a bezel or a low, secure profile, so the stone is guarded from daily knocks. One care note: skip the ultrasonic cleaner and harsh chemicals, and this is a ring you wear for decades. Built to last a lifetime is the standard here, same as every Bohemi ring.

More stone, not less

Why do salt-and-pepper diamonds cost less than white diamonds?

Salt-and-pepper diamonds live outside the commodity white-diamond market, so your budget goes further: you can often have a larger, more characterful stone than a colorless diamond of the same spend. They also carry no GIA grading report, and that is not a gap. That system was built to score eye-clean, colorless stones, not a diamond whose whole character is what sits inside it. What you get instead is a diamond valued for exactly what makes it individual.

How do you design a ring around a salt-and-pepper diamond?

Because the stone leads, we start there. We source and select your salt-and-pepper diamond with you, then design the entire ring around its pattern, its shape, and the way it catches light, so the setting serves the stone rather than fighting it. Some patterns want a clean bezel that frames them like art. Some want prongs that let light move through. That decision is part of the design. Some salt-and-pepper diamonds pair beautifully with textured bands and hidden details, the small design touches only the wearer knows are there, while others are at their best in a minimal setting that lets the stone do the talking.

Questions people ask first

Are salt-and-pepper diamonds real diamonds?
Yes. A salt-and-pepper diamond is a genuine diamond, chemically identical to a colorless one and just as hard at a 10 on the Mohs scale. The difference is the visible inclusions, the flecks of black, white, red, orange and brown that make each stone one of a kind.
Can I design a completely custom salt-and-pepper ring?
Yes. We source your stone with you, then design the whole ring around its pattern and shape. You bring the feeling and a few images, and we translate that into a real design built to last.
Does a salt-and-pepper diamond come with a certificate?
Not in the way a classic white diamond does, and that is by design, not by omission. Standard grading reports are built around the 4Cs, which assume a colorless, eye-clean stone. A salt-and-pepper diamond, with its visible inclusions and its own natural color, does not fit that scale, so there is no grade to issue. What you get instead is a diamond valued for what makes it individual, not scored against a standard it was never trying to meet.
How do I care for a salt-and-pepper diamond ring?
Skip the ultrasonic cleaner and harsh chemicals. Beyond that it is a ring you simply wear, for decades.
How long does a salt-and-pepper diamond engagement ring take to make?
It depends on how custom you go. A ready-made design takes about 4 to 6 weeks, made-to-measure about 6 to 8, and a fully bespoke piece about 8 to 12.
